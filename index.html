Instructor Story

Tyler Kim is a 4th Degree Black Belt in Taekwondo with over 16 years of martial arts training and experience. He brings high energy, enthusiasm, and a positive attitude to every class, creating an environment where students feel motivated, supported, and excited to challenge themselves.

Tyler believes Taekwondo is about more than kicking and punching. His teaching focuses on self-defense, honesty, respect, discipline, and strong listening skills while helping students build confidence, develop character, and grow both on and off the mat.

Whether working with young beginners or advanced students, Tyler is dedicated to helping each student reach their full potential and turn every class into a positive experience that inspires growth, perseverance, and success inside and outside the dojang.
