Instructor Story

I am a 4th Degree Black Belt in Taekwondo with over 16 years of martial arts training and experience. I am passionate about helping students grow both on and off the mat, and I bring high energy, enthusiasm, and a positive attitude to every class I teach.

I am known for my fun, energetic, and friendly teaching style, creating an engaging learning environment where students feel motivated, supported, and excited to challenge themselves. I believe that martial arts is about much more than kicking and punching - it’s about building confidence, developing character, and learning valuable life skills that students can carry with them every day.

My focus is on teaching self-defense, honesty, respect, discipline, and strong listening skills while helping students develop the confidence to overcome obstacles and achieve their goals. Whether I am working with young beginners or advanced students, I am dedicated to helping each individual reach their full potential.

Through my passion for Taekwondo and commitment to my students, I strive to make every class a positive experience that inspires growth, perseverance, and success both inside and outside the dojang.

MASTER KIM · Founder
